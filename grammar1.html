1. Core Syntax

Attributive Clause (定语从句)

Restrictive vs Non-restrictive, Relative pronouns/adverbs, Prep + Which/Whom

Priority: High

Key Rules & Syntax Analysis (语法详解与易错陷阱)

English Concept & Rules:

An attributive clause modifies a noun or pronoun (the antecedent). Relative pronouns (who, whom, whose, that, which) or relative adverbs (when, where, why) introduce the clause. Non-restrictive clauses add extra information, are separated by commas, and cannot use that.

中文解析与易错陷阱 (Rules & Common Traps):

分析方法：先找先行词，再分析从句成分（缺主语/宾语用代词，缺状语用副词）。
高频陷阱：① 非限制性定语从句（有逗号）及介词后绝对不能使用 that；② 介词提前时仅能用 prep + which (物) 或 prep + whom (人)。

// Pattern Formulas

1. Restrictive: Antecedent + relative pronoun/adverb + clause

2. Non-restrictive: Antecedent, + which/who/whose + clause (No "that")

3. Prepositional: Preposition + which/whom + clause

Annotated Examples (示范例句)

1. The country which/that I visited last year was beautiful. (Restrictive - object of "visited")

2. Mo Yan, whose novels are world-famous, won the Nobel Prize. (Non-restrictive - possessive)

3. This is the school in which / where my father taught English. (Adverbial of place)

Interactive Practice (巩固练习) Click toggle button to check answer and breakdown

Q1. Complete the sentence: London, _______ I spent my childhood, has changed a lot in recent years.

Answer: where / in which

Analysis: The antecedent is "London" (a place), and it serves as an adverbial of place in the subordinate clause (I spent my childhood in London). Because it is a non-restrictive clause with a comma, use relative adverb where or in which. That cannot be used after a comma.

Q2. Correct the error: The manager is the person to that you should apologize.

Answer: Change "to that" to "to whom"

Analysis: When a preposition precedes a relative pronoun referring to a person, whom must be used instead of that or who.

2. Core Verb Patterns

Non-finite Verbs (非谓语动词)

Infinitive (to do), Participles (-ing / -ed), Perfect & Passive forms

Priority: High

Key Rules & Syntax Analysis (语法详解与易错陷阱)

English Concept & Rules:

Non-finite verbs do not serve as main predicate verbs. Infinitives (to do) indicate purpose or future action; Present Participles (-ing) express active voice or ongoing action; Past Participles (-ed) express passive voice or completed state.

中文解析与易错陷阱 (Rules & Common Traps):

分析方法：找非谓语动词的逻辑主语（通常为主句主语），判断主动与被动关系。
高频陷阱：① 动作发生在主句动词之前时，必须用完成式 having done / having been done；② 谨防“悬垂分词”（非谓语逻辑主语与主句主语不一致导致语意错误）。

// Pattern Formulas

1. Present Participle: Doing (Active / Ongoing)

2. Past Participle: Done (Passive / Completed)

3. Perfect Participle: Having done / Having been done (Prior Action)

Annotated Examples (示范例句)

1. Hearing the bell, the students entered the classroom. (Active & Simultaneous action)

2. Having finished his assignment, Tom went out to play. (Active & Prior action)

3. Encouraged by the teacher, she made great progress. (Passive relationship with "she")

Interactive Practice (巩固练习)

Q1. Complete: _______ (see) from the top of the hill, the city looks magnificent.

Answer: Seen

Analysis: The logical subject of the participle is "the city". Since "the city" is seen (passive relationship), the past participle Seen must be used.

Q2. Complete: (Looking / Having looked) at the map for ten minutes, he finally found the destination.

Answer: Having looked

Analysis: Looking at the map lasted 10 minutes and was completed prior to finding the destination. Therefore, the perfect participle form Having looked is required.

3. Tenses

Tense Contrast (时态对比)

Present Perfect vs Past Simple, Present Perfect Continuous, Past Perfect

Priority: High

Key Rules & Syntax Analysis (语法详解与易错陷阱)

English Concept & Rules:

Present Perfect (have done) connects past actions to present results. Past Simple refers strictly to completed past events with no present link. Present Perfect Continuous (have been doing) stresses ongoing duration up to now. Past Perfect (had done) denotes an action completed before a specific past time ("past-in-the-past").

中文解析与时间标志词 (Time Indicators):

• Past Simple: yesterday, ago, in 2015, last week.
• Present Perfect: so far, since, for 3 years, recently, up to now.
• Past Perfect: by the time, before (past point), had + p.p.

Annotated Examples (示范例句)

1. I have lived in Beijing for 5 years. (Still living here now)

2. I lived in Beijing for 5 years. (Completed past event; no longer lives there)

3. By the time we arrived, the train had already left. (Past-in-the-past)

Interactive Practice (巩固练习)

Q1. Complete: Up to now, scientists _______ (discover) hundreds of exoplanets.

Answer: have discovered

Analysis: The time marker "up to now" signals present perfect tense, linking past discoveries to current scientific knowledge.

4. Voice

Passive Voice (被动语态)

Simple Passive, Present Continuous Passive (am/is/are being done), Perfect Passive

Priority: High

Key Rules & Syntax Analysis (语法详解与易错陷阱)

English Concept & Rules:

Passive voice emphasizes the recipient of an action (Subject + be + Past Participle). Present Continuous Passive takes the structure be + being + done.

中文解析与易错陷阱 (Rules & Common Traps):

关键陷阱：不及物动词（如 happen, take place, occur, rise, remain, appear）没有被动语态！例如：“事故发生了”应为 An accident happened (不用 was happened)。

Annotated Examples (示范例句)

1. A new stadium is being constructed near the park. (Present continuous passive)

2. Great achievements have been made in recent years. (Present perfect passive)

Interactive Practice (巩固练习)

Q1. Rewrite in passive form: "Engineers are repairing the server right now."

Answer: The server is being repaired by engineers right now.

Analysis: Convert to present continuous passive structure: Subject + is/are + being + past participle.

5. Complex Clauses

Noun Clauses (名词性从句)

Object, Subject, Predicative, Appositive Clause (Appositive vs Attributive Clause)

Priority: High

Key Rules & Syntax Analysis (语法详解与易错陷阱)

English Concept & Rules:

Noun clauses function as syntactic nouns (Subject, Object, Predicative, Appositive). Appositive clauses elaborate on the content of abstract nouns (fact, news, idea, promise, belief, thought).

核心对比：同位语从句 VS 定语从句

• 同位语从句：that 仅为连接词，在从句中不充当成分，完整解释前面抽象名词的内容。
• 定语从句：that 是关系代词，在从句中充当主语或宾语，对主词进行修饰限定。

Annotated Examples (示范例句)

1. The news that our team won the championship cheered everyone up. (Appositive clause - complete clause)

2. The news that he shared yesterday was surprising. (Attributive clause - 'that' acts as object)

Interactive Practice (巩固练习)

Q1. Identify clause type: "There is no doubt that artificial intelligence will transform industries."

Answer: Appositive Clause (同位语从句)

Analysis: The clause "that AI will transform industries" is complete and explains the exact nature of "doubt". That plays no syntactic role inside the clause.

6. Adverbials

Adverbial Clauses (状语从句)

Time, condition, concession, result, purpose; Primary-Future Sub-Present rule

Priority: Medium

Key Rules & Syntax Analysis (语法详解与易错陷阱)

English Concept & Rules:

Adverbial clauses modify verbs or main clauses, indicating time (as soon as, when), condition (if, unless), concession (although, even though), or purpose (so that).

核心法则：“主将从现” (Sequence of Tenses):

在时间与条件状语从句中，主句若为一般将来时，从句必须用一般现在时表达将来含义。
Example: We will set off as soon as he arrives.

Interactive Practice (巩固练习)

Q1. Complete: We will cancel the outdoor activity if it _______ (rain) tomorrow.

Answer: rains

Analysis: In conditional clauses introduced by "if", use present simple tense (rains) when the main clause uses future simple (will cancel).

7. Mood

Subjunctive Mood (虚拟语气)

Hypothetical conditionals, Wish, Suggest/Demand, It's high time...

Priority: Medium

Key Rules & Syntax Analysis (语法详解与易错陷阱)

Conditional Patterns:

• Present Unreal: If + did/were, S + would/could + do.
• Past Unreal: If + had done, S + would/could + have done.
• Future Unreal: If + did/should do/were to do, S + would + do.

特殊虚拟句型 (Special Subjunctive Forms):

① 建议/要求动词 (suggest, insist, order, demand) 后的宾语从句动词用 (should) + do；
② It's high time that + S + did / should do.

Annotated Examples (示范例句)

1. If I were you, I would accept the offer. (Present unreal)

2. If you had worked harder, you would have passed. (Past unreal)

Interactive Practice (巩固练习)

Q1. Complete: If she _______ (know) about the meeting yesterday, she would have attended.

Answer: had known

Analysis: The clause refers to past unreal conditions (marked by "yesterday"), requiring the past perfect form had known.

8. Modals

Modal Verbs & Speculation (情态动词推测与情感)

must / can't / should / might + have done

Priority: Medium

Key Rules & Syntax Analysis (语法详解与易错陷阱)

Deduction & Regret Structures:

• must have done: High-certainty positive deduction about past.
• can't have done: High-certainty negative deduction about past.
• should have done: Expressing regret (should have, but didn't).

中文解析：推测语气强度对比

must have done (肯定100%推测) > may/might have done (不确定推测) vs can't have done (不可能/否定推测)。

Interactive Practice (巩固练习)

Q1. Complete: The ground is completely wet. It _______ (must / should) have rained overnight.

Answer: must

Analysis: Based on concrete evidence ("ground is wet"), express high-certainty positive past deduction with must have rained.

9. Agreement

Subject-Verb Concord (主谓一致)

Grammatical, Notional, and Proximity Principles

Priority: Medium

Key Rules & Principles (三大原则)

1. Grammatical Concord

Form matches form. Singular subjects take singular verbs.

2. Notional Concord

Collective nouns (family, team): singular if seen as a unit, plural if seen as individual members.

3. Proximity Principle

either...or, neither...nor, not only...but also agree with the nearest subject.

Interactive Practice (巩固练习)

Q1. Choose correct verb: Neither the teacher nor the students _______ (was / were) aware of the schedule change.

Answer: were

Analysis: Follow the proximity rule for "neither...nor". The verb agrees with the closer subject "the students" (plural), requiring were.

10. Word Order

Inversion (倒装句)

Partial Inversion: Negative adverbs, "Only + adverbial"

Priority: Medium

Key Rules & Syntax Analysis (语法详解与易错陷阱)

Partial Inversion Formula:

Place auxiliary verb / modal verb before the main subject.
Trigger Word + Auxiliary + Subject + Main Verb

Common Inversion Triggers:

① Negative adverbs at front: Never, Seldom, Hardly, Little, Barely.
② Only + prepositional phrase / adverbial clause at front.

Interactive Practice (巩固练习)

Q1. Rewrite starting with "Seldom": "I seldom witness such dedicated enthusiasm."

Answer: Seldom do I witness such dedicated enthusiasm.

Analysis: Fronting the negative adverb "Seldom" triggers partial inversion, inserting auxiliary do before subject I.

11. Emphatic Sentences (强调句)

It is/was... that/who...

Formula: It is/was + [Emphasized Element] + that/who + [Remainder of Sentence].

Test method: If removing "It is/was... that" leaves a complete, grammatical sentence, it is an emphatic sentence.

Q1. Emphasize the underlined location: "I met Dr. Smith in the laboratory yesterday."

Answer: It was in the laboratory that I met Dr. Smith yesterday.

Wrap the prepositional phrase "in the laboratory" with "It was... that".

12. Ellipsis (省略句)

Adverbial & Infinitive Ellipsis

Rule: In adverbial clauses, if the subject matches the main clause subject and is followed by a be verb, "Subject + be" can be omitted.
Example: While (he was) crossing the street, he noticed the sign.

Q1. Simplify clause: "When he was asked about the incident, he remained silent."

Answer: When asked about the incident, he remained silent.

Omit identical subject "he" and verb "was".

13. Comparison & Articles (比较级与冠词)

Advanced Determiners & Multipliers
Multiplier Patterns

• A is 3 times as big as B.
• A is 3 times bigger than B.
• A is 3 times the size of B.

Definite Article (The) Rules

Use the for unique entities, superlatives, musical instruments (play the piano), and specific groups.

Q1. Complete comparison: "This new engine is three times _______ (efficient) than the old model."

Answer: more efficient

Follow the comparative pattern: [Multiplier] + comparative degree + than.
